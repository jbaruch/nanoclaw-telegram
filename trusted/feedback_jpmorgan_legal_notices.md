# J.P. Morgan Legal Notices — Do Not Report

J.P. Morgan (and brokerage accounts generally) sends mandatory legal/regulatory documents (ADV2B, broker disclosures, etc.) to each of Baruch's ~15 brokerage accounts. These arrive 1-5 at a time and are legally required, not actionable.

**Heartbeat rule:** Do NOT report J.P. Morgan "new online notice" emails. They are routine regulatory filings, not banking alerts or draw requests.

**Exception:** Still report actual banking action items — construction draws, payment failures, account alerts, fraud notices.
