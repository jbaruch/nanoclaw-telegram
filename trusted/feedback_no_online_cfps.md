Never include online or virtual conferences in CFP suggestions in the morning brief.

**Why:** Baruch only does in-person conferences.

**How to apply:** When listing upcoming CFP deadlines, filter out any conference labeled "Online", "Virtual", "Remote", or similar. In-person only.
