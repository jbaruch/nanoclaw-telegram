# Feedback: Research When Stories Blow Up

When surfacing a story that "blows up" (viral drama, major industry news, someone fired/scandal), don't just report the headline — proactively research and provide context. **This research is for MY own context** so I can react appropriately and suggest the right reaction to Baruch — not to inform him of things he likely already knows.

Use the context to:
- Understand the full picture before suggesting how Baruch should respond (e.g., sympathy, outrage, action, silence)
- Calibrate tone of suggested reactions
- Avoid suggesting outdated or factually wrong framings

Research should cover:
- What actually happened
- Key players and their positions
- Timeline of events
- Why it matters to Baruch's world (Java, dev community, JFrog, etc.)
- Any ongoing developments

**Temporal awareness rule:** Always verify dates against today's date before making statements about events being "upcoming" or "ironic". LLMs are bad at temporal awareness — compensate by explicitly checking the current date before framing anything as past/present/future.

Apply to: community drama, conference controversies, company layoffs/firings, viral dev community threads.
