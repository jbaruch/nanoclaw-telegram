# Robinhood — Shareholder Vote Emails

Robinhood shareholder voting notifications (annual meetings, proxy votes) are NOT actionable. Baruch is not interested in these.

**Do NOT flag in heartbeat or email triage.**

Examples to suppress:
- "Vote in the [Company] Annual Meeting"
- Any Robinhood email about shareholder proxy voting / annual meeting deadlines
