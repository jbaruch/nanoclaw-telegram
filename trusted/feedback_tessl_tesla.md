When Baruch says "Tesla" in a voice message and the context is about tiles, agents, skills, or AI tooling → he means **Tessl** (the tile system platform, tessl.io or similar).

When context is about cars, EVs, or Elon Musk → he means Tesla the car company.

**Why:** Voice transcriber reliably mistranscribes "Tessl" as "Tesla". This will happen every time.

**How to apply:** Never correct Baruch for "Tesla" in voice messages when context is clearly about agent tooling. Just treat it as Tessl silently.
